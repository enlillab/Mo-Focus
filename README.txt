MO FOCUS — Pomodoro timer made for Mohammad
===========================================

ON A COMPUTER (no install)
  Unzip this folder and double-click index.html.
  It opens in your browser and works offline.

ON A PHONE (install as an app)
  1. Put this folder online for free: drag it onto https://app.netlify.com/drop
     (or GitHub Pages). You get a link.
  2. Open the link on your phone.
     iPhone:  Safari > Share > Add to Home Screen
     Android: Chrome > menu > Install app
  It then opens full-screen like a normal app and works offline.

NOTES
  - Settings, tasks and statistics are saved on each device separately.
  - Alerts play while the app is open. Keep the volume up.
  - Space bar starts or pauses the timer on a computer.
